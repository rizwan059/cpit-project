Who We Are

About Tabeer Foundation for Change

Tabeer Foundation for Change was born from a simple yet powerful belief: real change begins when hope reaches the roots of society . In Pakistan’s vast rural landscapes—where millions of families rely on land, livestock, and small enterprises for survival—opportunity often remains out of reach. Tabeer Foundation exists to bridge this gap.

Registered under Section 42 of the Companies Act with SECP, Tabeer Foundation for Change is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering underserved and marginalized communities through sustainable socio-economic development. With nearly 38% of Pakistan’s workforce dependent on the rural economy, the foundation places rural livelihoods at the heart of its mission.

From a farmer struggling to sustain his crops, to a rural woman dreaming of turning her skills into a livelihood, Tabeer Foundation works closely with communities to transform challenges into opportunities. By promoting sustainable agriculture, livestock development, cottage industries, and rural enterprises, the foundation enables individuals not just to survive—but to thrive with dignity.

Through innovation, community participation, and strategic partnerships, Tabeer Foundation for Change strives to break the cycle of poverty, create meaningful employment, and nurture self-reliant communities. Each initiative is guided by empathy, inclusivity, and the conviction that lasting impact comes from empowering people to shape their own futures.

Our Vision

A Pakistan where every individual—regardless of geography or background—has equal access to opportunities, . sustainable livelihoods, and the power to build a dignified, prosperous life for themselves and their communities.

Our Mission

To alleviate poverty and drive inclusive rural development by:

  • Empowering rural communities through sustainable agriculture, livestock, and enterprise development
  • Creating income-generating opportunities that promote self-reliance and resilience
  • Supporting cottage industries and small businesses to strengthen local economies
